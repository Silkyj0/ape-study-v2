# Module-by-Module Contract Integration QA

Reviewed: **10 September 2026**

## Purpose

This record documents the M1–M13 QA pass undertaken after integrating the supplied **ABIC SW 2018 Simple Works Contract** and **CAA2024 Client Architect Agreement**.

The review applies four rules:

1. existing Acumen/PARCS content keeps its original primary source;
2. ABIC/CAA2024 is added to a core item only where the contract directly supports the proposition being tested;
3. confirmed PARCS wording and keys are not rewritten;
4. uncertainty or merely thematic relevance is not enough for a contract cross-reference.

M12 and M13 remain supplementary modules and are not treated as official PARCS modules.

## M1 — Architects, the Building Industry and Professional Liability

**Additional source relevance:** high for CAA2024.

**QA outcome:** materially refined.

- Cross-referenced the active CAA2024 questions dealing with G2 liability, Cost of Works / Total Project Cost, Schedule A, copyright and K2 termination.
- Added CAA2024 references to the standard-of-care, limitation-of-liability and PI flashcards.
- Corrected `m1-caa-costofworks`: the previous wording conflated the **defined Cost of Works** with the **forecast of Cost of Works**. The revised item distinguishes the Section M definition from the forecast recorded on the signing page and from Total Project Cost.
- Refined `m1-limitation-g2` to use the actual G2 mechanism: maximum aggregate liability is generally tied to the **net amount actually recovered under PI**, subject to G2 qualifications and applicable law.
- Refined Schedule A, F1 copyright/licence and K2 termination explanations using the signed agreement.

**Stable IDs:** preserved.

## M5 — Responsible Design: Country, Environment, Equity, Safety, Amenity

## M11 — Final Certificate, Dispute Resolution + Termination

**Additional source relevance:** high from both contracts, but for different relationships.

**QA outcome:** dual-contract reinforcement with explicit separation.

ABIC is used for the owner–contractor relationship:

- N11/N14 final certificate and its effect;
- C7 final security release;
- M1 completion relationship;
- P3 mediation/dispute process where directly applicable.

CAA2024 is used for the architect–client relationship:

- J2 fee/dispute pathway;
- D8 written fee/scope change mechanism.

This distinction is deliberate so a learner does not apply CAA2024 fee/dispute rules to the building contract or ABIC mechanisms to the architect's own professional-services agreement.

## Cross-module QA safeguards

A new production gate, `scripts/audit-contract-integration.mjs`, checks that:

- ABIC/CAA2024 question cross-references point to **active** study questions;
- flashcard cross-references point to real cards;
- contract precision overrides do not alter `parcs-confirmed` items;
- M12/M13 retain their source-verified statuses;
- supplementary statuses are absent from `EXAM_QA_STATUSES`;
- core M1–M11 material is not silently reclassified as contract-authored.

The review itself caught and removed archived/tangential references before release.
